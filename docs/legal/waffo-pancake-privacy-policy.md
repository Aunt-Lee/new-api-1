# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 2026-08-04**

This Privacy Policy explains how NewtonRouter ("NewtonRouter", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, discloses, and protects personal information when you use https://newtonrouter.com and its API gateway services (the "Service").

## 1. Data controller and contact

The data controller for the Service is:

- Privacy email: **newtonrouter@gmail.com**
- General support: **newtonrouter@gmail.com**

## 2. Information we collect

### 2.1 Information you provide

Depending on how you use the Service, we may collect your account email address, username, password hash, account preferences, API-key metadata, support messages, and information you provide when requesting a refund or account deletion.

For payments, we receive transaction identifiers, amount, currency, payment status, and limited customer details needed to reconcile an order. **Waffo Pancake and its payment partners process payment-card details; we do not intentionally store complete card numbers or card security codes on our servers.**

When you use the API gateway, your request may include prompts, files, images, audio, tool inputs, or other content. That content is processed to relay the request and response. It may be transmitted to the third-party model provider selected for the request.

### 2.2 Information collected automatically

We may collect IP address, device and browser information, operating system, language and time zone, timestamps, request identifiers, API route, selected model, channel, token and quota usage, latency, status codes, error summaries, and security or abuse signals.

Our standard usage records are intended to contain billing and operational metadata rather than full prompt and response content. We do not use your API inputs or outputs to train our own models. Third-party model providers may process request content under their own terms and privacy policies.

### 2.3 Cookies and local storage

We use strictly necessary cookies or browser storage to keep you signed in, protect sessions, remember language and interface preferences, and provide core functionality. We do not use advertising cookies as part of the Service unless this policy is updated to identify them.

## 4. How information is shared

We share information only as needed for the purposes described above:

- **Waffo Pancake and payment partners:** to create and reconcile payments, prevent fraud, and provide checkout services. Card data is handled by the payment provider and is not stored on our servers.
- **Third-party AI model providers:** when you request a model through the gateway. The provider receives the request data needed to generate the response and may process it under its own terms and privacy policy.
- **Infrastructure and service providers:** hosting, database, cache, email, monitoring, security, and support providers that process information on our instructions and under appropriate confidentiality obligations.
- **Legal and safety recipients:** courts, regulators, law enforcement, auditors, or advisers when disclosure is required or reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety, the Service, or other users.
- **Business transactions:** a successor or professional adviser in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets, subject to appropriate confidentiality and notice requirements.

We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

## 5. Third-party AI processing

NewtonRouter routes API requests to the model provider and channel selected by you or by the configured routing policy. The selected provider may store, review, or otherwise process inputs and outputs according to its own policy, retention settings, and legal obligations. You should review the provider's terms before sending confidential or regulated data.

NewtonRouter does not intentionally use your request content to train its own models. This statement does not change how a third-party provider processes data after it receives a request.

## 6. Security

We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the risk, including HTTPS/TLS in transit, access controls, password hashing, restricted administrative access, API-key protections, operational monitoring, and backups where necessary for recovery. No transmission or storage system is completely secure.

If we determine that a security incident creates a legally reportable risk, we will notify affected individuals and regulators within the time required by applicable law. Where GDPR notification duties apply, we target notification to the relevant supervisory authority within 72 hours after becoming aware of a qualifying breach.

## 7. Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes in this policy, account operation, security, dispute handling, and legal or accounting obligations.

Our intended baseline retention periods are:

| Data category | Intended retention | Disposal |
|---|---|---|
| Account and authentication data | While the account is active; normally deleted or anonymized within 90 days after verified account deletion, unless law or an unresolved dispute requires longer | Deletion or anonymization |
| Payment and transaction records | For the period required by applicable tax, accounting, and payment rules, normally up to 7 years | Secure deletion or archival access restriction |
| Usage and reliability metadata | Normally up to 90 days, unless needed for a security investigation or legal claim | Secure deletion or aggregation |
| Support and privacy requests | Normally up to 3 years after resolution | Secure deletion or anonymization |
| API inputs and outputs | Transient processing; not intentionally retained as standard usage records by NewtonRouter | Deletion after processing, subject to technical caching or provider processing outside our control |

## 8. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, deletion of, restriction of, or portability of your personal information, and to object to processing based on legitimate interests or direct marketing. Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw consent at any time.

Send a request to **newtonrouter@gmail.com** from the account email when possible. We may need to verify your identity before responding. We aim to respond within 30 calendar days, or within the shorter period required by applicable law. You may also complain to the data-protection authority in your jurisdiction.

## 9. Marketing communications

We send optional marketing communications only where permitted and, where required, after obtaining consent. You may unsubscribe using the link in a marketing email, by changing your account preferences, or by contacting us. Unsubscribing does not stop essential service, payment, security, or legal notices.

## 10. International transfers

NewtonRouter, Waffo Pancake, infrastructure providers, and third-party model providers may process information in countries other than your own. Where required by applicable law, we use an approved transfer mechanism such as an adequacy decision, standard contractual clauses, or another legally recognized safeguard.

## 11. Children

The Service is intended for people aged 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18. If you believe a child has provided information to us, contact **newtonrouter@gmail.com** and we will investigate and delete it where required.

## 12. Third-party services and links

The Service may link to or integrate with third-party services, including Waffo Pancake and AI model providers. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies. This policy applies only to information collected by NewtonRouter.

## 13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our Service, providers, or legal obligations change. For material changes, we will provide reasonable notice through the Service or by email and update the "Last updated" date. The new version applies from its stated effective date.
